## Rule: separate face DNA before style

For each recurring agent, lock distinct ordinary real-life traits before generating:

- age band
- face shape
- eye shape and spacing
- brows
- nose shape
- lip shape
- skin tone and texture
- moles, dimples, acne marks, under-eye warmth, faint lines
- hair length, part, texture, frizz, gray strands, flyaways
- posture and energy

Use average beauty bans explicitly:

- avoid AI-slop average beauty
- do not make a generic K-drama face
- ordinary real-life Korean office worker
- natural asymmetry, pores, mild under-eye warmth, faint blemishes
- no plastic skin, no glamour gloss, no symmetrical perfection

## Workflow

1. Generate base anchors first, not episode scenes.
2. Create at least 2 candidates for critical characters.
3. Make a contact sheet and compare same-gender faces side by side.
4. Reject if the silhouettes, eye/nose/lip geometry, and skin texture feel interchangeable.
5. Only after approval, create outfit sheets, expression sheets, and episode scenes from the chosen anchors.

## QA prompt

When reviewing a contact sheet, ask:

- Can I identify each character by face alone without reading the label?
- Do male characters differ in face shape, eye shape, nose, hair texture, and age energy?
- Do female characters differ beyond hairstyle and outfit?
- Does anyone look like a generic AI-generated office worker?
- Are there over-smoothed skin, symmetrical perfection, or K-drama average-beauty signals?
