## Durable rule

Do not solve character separation by making faces harsh, tired, overly asymmetrical, or “anti-beauty.” Keep the first-direction polish and separate characters mostly through:

- hair silhouette and styling
- wardrobe category and color
- role-specific props
- posture / expression energy
- environment and work context
- subtle facial variation only

## Prompt language to avoid in final image prompts

Avoid abstract worldbuilding terms that do not produce visible pixels and may push the model toward concept art:

- `Seo-Ian's lean company universe`
- `humanized AI agent employee`
- `AI agent character`
- `startup universe`
- metaphorical satire language

These may belong in planning notes, not the generation prompt.

## Translate role into visible cues

Instead of writing `Build Lab verification agent`, translate to visual cues:

- MacBook with blurred logs or issue tracker
- quiet focused posture
- minimal charcoal/white outfit
- black tumbler / notebook
- natural Korean startup office light

Instead of writing `Creator agent`, translate to:

- phone, storyboard notes, caption drafts
- brighter casual outfit
- lively expression
- creator desk / small tripod in background only if natural

## Better prompt shape

Use visible production blocks:

1. Subject: realistic Korean office worker, age range, face/identity anchor.
2. Scene: small Korean startup office / candid iPhone office-vlog context.
3. Wardrobe/props: role-specific but natural.
4. Camera/light: iPhone/35mm equivalent, soft window light, realistic skin texture.
5. Constraints: no concept art, no character poster, no plastic skin, no identity drift, no text/watermark.

Keep worldbuilding in a separate planning note and only feed visible details into the generation prompt.
